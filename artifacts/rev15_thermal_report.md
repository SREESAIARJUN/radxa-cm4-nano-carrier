| operating, 90 deg adverse | 70.6 | 32.9 | 3.43e+03 | 490 | 326 | 48.0 | **32.9 W (capillary)** |

| cold start 20 C | 20.0 | 16.9 | 27.4 | 41.3 | 107 | 48.0 | **16.9 W (capillary)** |

Cold start: Q_max reaches 15 W at T_v = 16.0 C; from a 0 C soak the stalled evaporator (C = 4.8 J/K) self-heats there in 5.1 s at 15 W - far below the throttle time constant. water pipe: storage/operation below 0 C freezes the working fluid (sintered wicks tolerate freeze-thaw; cold start then follows this self-heating path).

Maximum adverse tilt (evaporator above condenser) keeping 1.5 x 15 W: **90 deg** at operating T_v; carrying 15 W from a 20 C soak: 90 deg.
